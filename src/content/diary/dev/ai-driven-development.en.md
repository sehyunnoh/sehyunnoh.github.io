I have been building things with [orca](https://www.onorca.dev/) lately, and it looks like I am adding about one project a week.

## Barely any time in, far more out

I am not putting in much time, and yet the quality is genuinely beyond what I had imagined. Work like this would not have fit into six months before. It took a month.

## Still getting the hang of it

I am very much still learning the tool, and there is a lot I am not good at yet. But if I keep at it, I think a process and a setup that actually fit me will come together.

## What's next — a home server

On Black Friday I want to pick up a low-power mini PC, set it up as a home server, and run orca on it so I can put AI to work properly.

- Install the [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/nousresearch/hermes-agent) and wire it to Discord, so everything in progress can be watched and steered from one place.
- Build an LLM wiki so the AI has a better picture of how I work.

## What a time

That I can turn what I want into something real, this quickly — I am grateful to be around for it.
