There is a sofa on the third floor, but it's so old and uncomfortable that we hardly ever go up there. After talking it over with my wife, we decided to replace it and visited a few furniture stores. We went to one place whose name I don't remember, The Brick, and Structube. We bought the sofa on the first floor at Structube too, and in the end we chose Structube again this time. The price was reasonable, and it's the sofa bed we wanted, so we picked it quickly without any real disagreement.
