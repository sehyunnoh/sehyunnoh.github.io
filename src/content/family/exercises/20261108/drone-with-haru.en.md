A few days ago, my wife and I were at Costco and spotted a drone. We're getting ready for a LEGO robotics competition, and our solution involves a drone, so I figured it'd be useful and bought it. But Haru didn't show much interest. I figured I'd just play with it myself, but once I gave Haru a mission, he got really into it.

I set up a destination using a chair or a foam roller and made landing there the goal, and he had a blast. I got to fly it too, and it wasn't easy — you have to think in three dimensions.

Whenever we have time, I should play with the drone with Haru like we did today.
