The weather's gotten noticeably cold. We could've just done something else at home, but Hana's been really into pickleball lately, so we all went out to the park in the evening to get some exercise.

Sangmi and Haru played basketball off to the side while Hana and I played pickleball, and she's clearly got good athletic instincts. Nobody's specifically taught her, but she picks things up quickly.

Pickleball's pretty popular these days — should I get her some proper coaching and start taking her to tournaments here and there? Haha. Though that probably won't be so easy either. For now, the goal is just to keep playing steadily whenever we get the chance.
