Paris Baguette finally opened in our neighbourhood today. There were times in Canada when I missed Korean bread, so this is wonderful news. T&T does sell similar-style bread, but compared to real Korean bakery bread it's still missing that last 2%. I hear a Shake Shack is opening right across the street next week, so I'll have to go on opening day for that one too.
