Today is my daughter's birthday. She wanted to go to an Italian restaurant, so we had lunch at East Side Mario's. The Burlington Centre location last time wasn't great and I wanted to try somewhere else, but she asked to go, so I followed along without a word. The Mississauga location turned out to have a better atmosphere and tastier food.
