Today was Haru's friend Emilio's birthday party. I dropped Sangmi and Haru off at Reynolds Community Center by 11. Hana and I each had a few things to take care of, so we stayed home first, left around 1, and joined the party late — then after it ended we headed into the community center to play pickleball.

The birthday party looked like a lot of fun. They seemed to be playing with Nerf guns, and everyone looked like they were having a blast.
